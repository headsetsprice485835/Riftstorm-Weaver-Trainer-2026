## 🎮 About the Game

RIFTSTORM is a cooperative PvE looter-shooter action RPG with roguelite elements, developed by Confiction Labs and Agate. It entered Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026. Players control elite Weavers, complete missions, fight Mythic threats, collect weapons and resources, develop builds with Lythograms, and extract with their rewards.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **RIFTSTORM**. This trainer provides configurable gameplay assistance for RIFTSTORM missions, including survivability, ammunition, reload, weapon handling, ability resources, and extraction protection. It is designed around the game's Weaver-based combat, weapon loadouts, escalating enemy encounters, in-mission Lythogram upgrades, and risk-reward extraction loop.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Weaver builds, test weapons and Lythogram combinations, practice against Mythic enemies, reduce mission repetition, or explore RIFTSTORM's extraction-focused progression at their own pace.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents the active Weaver from losing health during missions.
- **Infinite Mission Resources** — Keeps supported resource counters from decreasing during an active deployment.
- **Extraction Protection** — Reduces the consequences of failed extraction attempts during supported missions.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Keeps ammunition available for equipped weapons during firefights.
- **No Reload** — Removes normal reload interruptions while using supported weapons.
- **Rapid Fire** — Improves the firing rate of compatible weapons for faster enemy clearing.
- **Unlimited Ability Resource** — Maintains the resources required to use supported Weaver abilities.
- **Instant Ability Recovery** — Reduces or removes recovery time for supported abilities.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Select a Weaver and enter a mission normally. Move through the top-down combat arenas, avoid enemy attacks, and use the trainer's health option when practicing positioning or exploring unfamiliar mission layouts. Movement remains controlled through the game's normal keyboard and mouse controls.

### Tools & Inventory

Equip the weapons and loadout you want before deployment. Ammunition and reload options apply to supported weapons after the mission begins. Collect weapons, resources, Lythograms, and other rewards as usual, then extract to preserve mission progress and loot.

### World & Environment

RIFTSTORM missions combine curated and procedurally generated content with escalating Threat levels. Explore objectives, fight enemy groups, gather field rewards, and decide how long to remain deployed before extraction. Trainer options should be enabled selectively when testing builds or learning mission routes.

### Combat & Weapons

Use the combat options to stabilize health, ammunition, reload behavior, firing speed, and Weaver ability usage. Combine these tools with Lythogram upgrades to test different builds against Mythic enemies and larger hordes. Disable unnecessary options before extraction or when checking normal weapon and progression behavior.
